Continuous swimmer tracking

Edge-AI cameras use YOLO-class object detection and multi-object tracking to build a behavioral profile for every swimmer over time — not just single-frame snapshots. The system maintains individual tracks even in crowded pools with varying water conditions and surface glare.

YOLO-class detection + multi-object tracking running entirely on-device

Early distress detection

The system identifies warning signs before full submersion: agonal breathing, hair over the face, reduced or erratic limb motion, vertical sinking posture, and prolonged failure to resurface. Behavioral models trained on swimming and distress datasets from Roboflow, Figshare, and GitHub repositories.

Detects subtle distress signals faster than the average 30-second human recognition window

Instant, hands-free alert

An audible alarm and visual cue fire directly from the camera unit within 1–3 seconds — no phone required. The system pinpoints the exact pool zone so guards can respond immediately. On-camera alerts are critical: most facilities prohibit lifeguards from carrying phones on the guard stand.

Audio + visual alarm fires on-camera. App and Apple Watch for off-stand staff. 1–3 second response.

The Brave Waters app shows a live pool map with swimmer icons, zone activity, and incident history — available on iPhone and Apple Watch. Brave Waters is an assistive tool. It does not replace certified lifeguards or legal supervision requirements.
